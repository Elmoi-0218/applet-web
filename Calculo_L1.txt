LIGHTNING PERFORMANCE
 Double circuit line with 1 shield wire 
 Span length: 250 m
 Ground Flash Density: 10.0 flashes per square km per year
 Tower Surge Impedance: 200 ohm
 Wave velocity down the tower: 300 meter/microsecond
 Strike Distance Equation used: Strike Distance = 10.0*h^0.00*I^0.65
        (h is the height of the wire, I is the current of the stroke)
 Account is made for soil ionization using Korsuncev's method with: 
          Soil resistivity: 400 ohm meter
          Soil critical ionization gradient: 400 kV/m
          Parameter S: 2.0 m
